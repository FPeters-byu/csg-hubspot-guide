The Golden Data Rule: 4 Linked CRM Objects

Core Database Logic

Everything in HubSpot revolves around four connected record types. Never store client updates in private spreadsheets or personal Gmail threads—always link records together:

Contacts (People)

Individual people (CEOs, VPs, Alumni). Tracked by email address. Use Contact Type (Prospective Client, Active Client, Past Client, CSG Alumni) to segment audiences.

Companies (Organizations)

The client organization (e.g., Alcomy). Automatically groups all contacts, deals, notes, and support tickets belonging to that business.

Deals (Projects & Revenue)

Tracks potential and active consulting engagements from Discovery Call to Closed Won ($2,500+ engagement scope, timeline, and deliverables).

Tickets (Service & Support)

Tracks ongoing client questions, data requests, scope changes, and post-project support items inside the Service Hub Help Desk.

The 3-Column Record Layout: Whenever you open any Contact, Company, Deal, or Ticket in HubSpot, the screen has three columns: Left Column (Properties): Fixed fields like Revenue, Industry, Health Score, and Milestone Status. Middle Column (Timeline): Chronological log of emails, Zoom summaries, Pinned Notes, and Tasks. Right Column (Associations): Connected contacts, companies, deals, tickets, and attached files (e.g., signed Engagement Letters).

1. Help Desk (Central Support & Ticket Workspace)

−
1 What is the Function & What Does It Do?
General Functionality

The Help Desk is a centralized, multi-channel workspace that aggregates incoming inquiries from shared team emails, website forms, live chat, and portals into structured, trackable support tickets. Beyond simple inbox management, it links every ticket simultaneously to CRM Contact, Company, and Deal records, enforces Service Level Agreements (SLAs) with automated countdown timers, routes issues based on custom rules, and includes Pro AI features (automated ticket summaries, AI reply suggestions, and automated ticket naming/routing).

CSG Specific Application & Real Examples
  • Zero Knowledge Loss Across Semesters: Replaces fragmented private student email inboxes with one shared team inbox (client-support@cougarstrategy.org). When cohorts change, the entire client communication history stays permanently attached to the Company record.
  • Scope-Change Tracking (e.g., Alcomy Project): When a client requests extra analysis mid-semester, logging it as a ticket documents whether the request falls inside or outside the signed Engagement Letter scope.
  • Data Delivery Follow-Ups: When waiting on client data, setting a ticket to Waiting on Client triggers an automatic reminder after 3–4 days so projects do not stall before Stage Gate reviews.
2 How to Implement, Use & Input Our Information (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Connect Team Inbox & Configure Ticket Pipeline
  1. Click the Settings gear icon (top right) → go to Inbox & Help Desk > Help Desk.
  2. Click Connect a channel → select Team Email, connect client-support@cougarstrategy.org, and turn on Automatic ticket creation for incoming emails.
  3. Navigate to Settings > Objects > Tickets > Pipelines and customize your stages to match CSG operations: New Request → Under Review → In Progress → Waiting on Client → Resolved / Closed.
Step 2: Configure SLAs & Pipeline Automation (Pro Features)
  1. Set SLAs: Go to Settings > Inbox & Help Desk > SLAs. Set Time to First Response = 24 hours (Monday–Friday) and Time to Close by priority, with an automated warning alert 3 hours before expiration.
  2. Add Pipeline Automation: Under the ticket pipeline Automate tab, add a rule: if a ticket stays in Waiting on Client for 3 business days without reply, automatically create a follow-up Task for the Ticket Owner.
  3. Create Text Snippets: Go to CRM > Snippets and save standard templates (e.g., #datarequest). Typing #datarequest inside any ticket reply inserts the full message automatically.
Step 3: Inputting Information & Daily Team Execution
  • Automated Input: Emails to the shared inbox or website support forms automatically create a ticket linked to the sender's Contact and Company.
  • Manual Input (Calls/Meetings): Open Service > Help Desk, click Create ticket (top right), enter a standardized name ([Client] – [Topic] – [Phase]), select Pipeline, Stage, and Priority (Low/Medium/High), and associate the Company and Contact.
  • Daily Execution: Filter by Unassigned each morning, assign a Ticket Owner, reply directly using the Reply tab (client-visible), or switch to the yellow Comment tab to tag teammates or Paul Godfrey with @Name internally.
Common Pitfall to Avoid: Never reply to client tickets from your personal university Gmail/Outlook account. If you do, the ticket stays open in Help Desk, the SLA timer turns red, and future semesters lose the reply. Always reply directly inside Help Desk.

2. Customer Success (Engagement Health & Escalation Control)

−
1 What is the Function & What Does It Do?
General Functionality

Customer Success is a strategic account-management framework designed to monitor ongoing client relationship health (Account Health Score), track structured onboarding and delivery milestones, and trigger early interventions when engagement risks appear—rather than reacting only after a client complains.

CSG Specific Application
  • Real-Time Leadership Visibility: Gives Faculty Advisor Paul Godfrey and VPs of Operations an immediate traffic-light overview (Green / Yellow / Red) of every active semester engagement before Stage Gate reviews.
  • Standardized Phase Tracking: Tracks progress across CSG's three standard consulting phases: Phase 1: Diagnostic Assessment, Phase 2: Strategy & Org Design, and Phase 3: Final SOP & Slide Deck.
Important License Note (Pro vs. Enterprise Workaround): The dedicated menu item Service > Customer Success and native automated health score calculations are Enterprise-only features. With CSG's Service Hub Professional license, we replicate this exact capability using Custom Company Properties + Saved CRM Views + Escalation Workflows (steps below).
2 How to Implement, Use & Input Our Information (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Create Custom Health & Milestone Properties
  1. Go to Settings > Properties and select Company Properties.
  2. Click Create property and create two standardized dropdown fields:
    • CSG Client Health Score (Dropdown options: Green - On Track, Yellow - At Risk, Red - Critical).
    • Project Milestone Status (Dropdown options: Kickoff Completed, Phase 1 Delivered, Phase 2 Delivered, Final Presentation Delivered).
  3. Set both fields as required during client onboarding so no active client record stays blank.
Step 2: Input Weekly Client Status Updates
  1. Open CRM > Companies and select your active client record (e.g., Alcomy).
  2. In the left sidebar under CSG Client Health, update the CSG Client Health Score and Project Milestone Status after every weekly client status call, then click Save.
  3. In the middle timeline, click Notes, log a 3-bullet summary of the weekly call, and click Actions > Pin note so leadership sees the latest status at the very top.
Step 3: Build Saved Risk Views & Escalation Workflows
  1. In CRM > Companies, click + Add view, filter by CSG Client Health Score is any of Yellow - At Risk, Red - Critical, and save it as At-Risk CSG Engagements.
  2. Under Automations > Workflows, build a Company-based workflow: whenever CSG Client Health Score changes to Yellow or Red, automatically create a high-priority Task for the VP of Operations to schedule an intervention call with the client sponsor.

3. Customer Agent (Breeze AI Support Assistant)

−
1 What is the Function & What Does It Do?
General Functionality

The Customer Agent (powered by HubSpot Breeze AI) is an autonomous conversational AI assistant trained exclusively on your approved knowledge sources (Knowledge Base articles, public website URLs, and uploaded PDFs). It answers visitor and client questions 24/7 across chat, email, and forms in natural language, and automatically triggers a Human Handoff (creating a Help Desk ticket) when a question requires human judgment.

CSG Specific Application
  • 24/7 Coverage Between Classes: Student consultants cannot monitor live chat during lectures. The Customer Agent instantly answers common prospect questions ("How long is a CSG engagement?", "What industries do you serve?", "How are projects priced?").
  • Internal Consultant Assistant: Can also assist new student consultants in quickly finding answers on CSG frameworks, templates, and SOP standards.
2 How to Implement, Use & Input Our Information (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Activate Agent & Connect Approved Knowledge Sources
  1. First, ensure at least 15–20 Knowledge Base articles (Module 5) are published.
  2. Navigate to Service > Customer Agent and click Get Started / Set up agent.
  3. Name the agent (e.g., CSG Virtual Assistant) and select a professional, helpful tone of voice.
  4. Under Knowledge / Data Sources, link:
    • Your published CSG Knowledge Base articles.
    • Public CSG website URLs (services, process, FAQ pages).
    • Approved public PDFs (e.g., CSG Capabilities Deck or Engagement Scope One-Pager—never upload confidential client deliverables).
Step 2: Configure Human Handoff & Escalation Rules
  1. Open the Handoff / Actions tab inside Customer Agent settings.
  2. Define the fallback rule when the AI cannot answer confidently or when a user clicks "Talk to a consultant": select Create a ticket in Help Desk and route it to the Business Development Lead.
  3. Enable email and in-app notifications so the team is alerted immediately when a prospect requests human follow-up.
Step 3: Weekly AI Training & Maintenance
  1. Once a week, open Service > Customer Agent > Unhandled Questions.
  2. Review questions the AI could not answer.
  3. Click Add answer to Knowledge Base next to valid questions, write the approved response, and save—the agent learns the new topic immediately.

4. Chatflows (Website Chat & Automated Lead Bots)

−
1 What is the Function & What Does It Do?
General Functionality

Chatflows is a visual drag-and-drop builder for website chat widgets. Teams can deploy either a real-time Live Chat or a rule-based Chatbot (if/then decision tree) that greets website visitors, asks qualifying questions, writes answers directly into CRM Contact/Company properties, and embeds calendar booking links inside the chat window.

CSG Specific Application
  • 24/7 Website Lead Qualification: Captures prospective business decision-makers visiting the CSG website, collects company details and project scope, and lets them book a 20-minute Discovery Call immediately.
  • Clean Visitor Routing: Separates website visitors automatically into Prospective Clients (Hire CSG), Students (Join CSG), and General/Alumni Inquiries so student applications do not clutter the client sales pipeline.
2 How to Implement, Use & Input Our Information (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Create Chatflow & Select Channel
  1. Navigate to Service > Chatflows (or Automations > Chatflows) and click Create chatflow.
  2. Select Website and choose Bot (automated decision-tree bot—recommended for student teams with rotating class schedules).
  3. Name the bot clearly (e.g., CSG Website Inbound Qualifier).
Step 2: Build Decision Tree, CRM Data Capture & Meeting Booking
  1. Welcome Message: Enter: "Welcome to Cougar Strategy Group! How can we help you today?"
  2. Quick-Reply Branching: Add three quick-reply buttons:
    • Button 1: Request a Consulting Project (Hire CSG)
    • Button 2: Apply as a Student Consultant (Join CSG)
    • Button 3: General Question
  3. CRM Property Mapping (Button 1 Path): Add sequential question nodes mapped directly to CRM fields:
    • "What is your first and last name?" → saves to First Name / Last Name.
    • "What is your business email?" → saves to Email (and sets Contact Type = Prospective Client).
    • "Which company do you represent?" → saves to Company Name.
  4. Meeting Booking Node: At the end of the client branch, add the Book a meeting action linked to the CSG Business Development Lead's calendar.
Step 3: Configure Targeting, Branding & Publish
  1. Open the Target tab and restrict the widget to public pages (e.g., homepage and /services, excluding internal pages).
  2. Open the Display tab, apply CSG Navy/Royal Blue branding, test the full flow internally, and toggle the switch at top right to On (Publish).

5. Knowledge Base (Self-Service Library & Internal SOP Hub)

−
1 What is the Function & What Does It Do?
General Functionality

The Knowledge Base is a structured, searchable online library for help articles, process guides, FAQs, and documentation. Articles are organized into categories, optimized for search engines (SEO/AEO), tracked via built-in readership analytics, and controlled per article as either Public or Private (Password-Protected / Registration Required).

CSG Specific Application (Dual Purpose)
  • External (Public Client Portal): Explains CSG's consulting methodology (e.g., 7Rs framework), project timelines, data confidentiality standards, and onboarding steps—building executive credibility and reducing repetitive emails.
  • Internal (Password-Protected Consultant SOPs): Acts as a permanent digital handbook for incoming CSG consultants (HubSpot tutorials, deliverable templates, Loom walkthroughs) so institutional knowledge never disappears.
2 How to Implement, Use & Input Our Information (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Configure Branding & Subdomain
  1. Navigate to Service > Knowledge Base.
  2. Click Customize template to upload the CSG logo and apply CSG Navy/Royal Blue colors.
  3. Under Settings > Domains & URLs, connect your dedicated subdomain (e.g., help.cougarstrategy.org).
Step 2: Create Categories & Write Structured Articles
  1. Click Manage categories and create clear folders:
    • Client Onboarding & Project Process (External)
    • CSG Consulting Frameworks & Methodologies (External)
    • Internal CSG SOPs & HubSpot Guides (Internal)
  2. Click Create article (top right), enter a clear question-based title (e.g., "What to Expect During a CSG Diagnostic Assessment Phase"), and use headings, bullet lists, screenshots, and embedded Loom/YouTube videos in the editor.
Step 3: Control Audience Access, Publish & Track Insights
  1. In the right sidebar under Control audience access, choose Public for client-facing articles or Private – Password required for internal consultant SOPs.
  2. Click Publish. Review the Insights tab monthly to see top-viewed articles and search terms with zero results (indicating new articles to write).

7. Feedback Surveys (Automated CSAT/NPS & Historical CSV Import)

−
1 What is the Function & What Does It Do?
General Functionality

An automated client-listening engine for creating, distributing, and analyzing CSAT (Customer Satisfaction), NPS (Net Promoter Score, 0–10 loyalty scale), CES (Customer Effort Score), and custom multi-question surveys via email, web link, or chat. Every rating and open-text comment writes directly to the respondent's Contact and Company record and can trigger follow-up workflows.

CSG Specific Application
  • Central Evidence Hub for Marketing Team 2: Consolidates all quantitative proof points (average NPS/CSAT) and quotable client testimonials in one CRM database instead of scattered Google Forms or Excel files.
  • Automated Testimonial & Recovery Loops: If a client submits an NPS score of 9 or 10 after final presentation, HubSpot automatically sends a thank-you email asking permission to feature their quote on the CSG website. Scores ≤ 6 immediately alert leadership for service recovery.
2 How to Implement, Use & Input Our Information (Step-by-Step)
Part A: Create & Automate New CSG Surveys
  1. Navigate to Service > Feedback Surveys and click Create survey.
  2. Select survey type: Customer satisfaction (CSAT) for milestone/project evaluation, Customer loyalty (NPS) for brand advocacy, or Custom survey.
  3. Choose delivery channel (Email for personalized tracking or Shareable link to embed on the final presentation slide).
  4. Customize questions and CSG branding under the Survey tab.
  5. Set Automated Triggers (Recipients / Automation Tab):
    • Post-Project Trigger: Send automatically 3 days after a Deal moves to Closed Won / Project Completed.
    • End-of-Semester Trigger: Trigger a scheduled workflow in Week 13 for all contacts with Engagement Status = Active.
Part B: How to Input Pre-Existing / Historical Surveys (CSV Import)
  1. Create Custom Contact Properties First: Go to Settings > Properties > Contact Properties and create:
    • Historical NPS Score (Field type: Number)
    • Past Project CSAT Rating (Field type: Dropdown 1–5)
    • Client Feedback & Testimonial Quote (Field type: Multi-line text)
    • Survey Completion Date (Field type: Date picker)
  2. Format Your Excel / Google Forms / Qualtrics File: Rename row 1 headers cleanly to: Email, First Name, Last Name, Company Name, Historical NPS Score, Client Feedback & Testimonial Quote, Survey Completion Date, and save as .csv (UTF-8).
  3. Import into HubSpot: Go to CRM > Contacts → click Import (top right) → File from computer > One file > One object > Contacts → upload your .csv, map each column to its matching HubSpot property, and click Finish import. HubSpot matches existing clients by email without creating duplicates.
Reporting Note for Imported CSV Surveys: CSV-imported survey values are stored as standard custom properties on Contact records—they do not populate HubSpot's native Survey Analytics charts. To chart historical scores alongside new surveys, build a Custom Report in Module 8 using your custom properties.

8. Service Analytics (Service Reporting & Executive Dashboards)

−
1 What is the Function & What Does It Do?
General Functionality

Service Analytics is HubSpot's dedicated service reporting suite featuring 20+ pre-built standard reports plus a Custom Report Builder. It visualizes ticket volume, SLA compliance, average Time to First Reply, average Time to Close, consultant workload, and CSAT/NPS trends over time.

CSG Specific Application
  • Hard ROI & Quality Proof for Paul Godfrey: Demonstrates to faculty leadership with objective numbers how quickly student teams respond to clients, how many deliverables are completed, and how satisfaction trends across semesters.
  • Segment Intelligence for Marketing Team 1: Cross-references NPS/CSAT scores with Target Client Segments (e.g., SaaS startups vs. mid-market firms) so CSG prioritizes the highest-satisfaction client industries.
2 How to Implement, Use & Input Our Information (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Explore & Filter Pre-Built Service Reports
  1. Navigate to Service > Service Analytics.
  2. Select a report category in the left sidebar:
    • Customer Outcomes: CSAT score over time, NPS distribution (Promoters vs. Passives vs. Detractors).
    • Response Times: Average Time to First Reply, Average Time to Close, SLA attainment rate.
    • Ticket Volume: Tickets created by channel, priority, or project phase.
  3. Use the global date filter at the top to isolate the current semester (e.g., This quarter / Last 90 days).
Step 2: Pin Key Metrics to the CSG Executive Leadership Dashboard
  1. Open a core report (e.g., Average Time to First Reply or NPS Score by Client Segment) and click Save report (top right).
  2. Select Add to existing dashboard and choose CSG Executive Leadership Dashboard.
  3. Repeat for your top 3 service KPIs so Paul Godfrey sees Marketing, Sales, and Service performance on one single executive screen.
Step 3: Automate Monthly Executive PDF Reports
  1. Open Reporting > Dashboards and select CSG Executive Leadership Dashboard.
  2. Click Share > Email this dashboard (top right).
  3. Enter the email addresses of Paul Godfrey and the CSG Team Leads, select Recurring – Monthly (1st of every month), choose PDF attachment, and click Schedule.
