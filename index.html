Ground Rules for every cohort

Shared governance standards that keep CSG’s CRM database clean, prevent duplicate records, and control software seat costs across rotating student semesters.

Seat Allocation & Cost Governance

  • Paid Specialist Seats: Assigned strictly to active Sales leads, Marketing Core leads (3 seats), and the Service Hub Pro lead (1 seat).
  • Free View-Only Seats: Assigned to all remaining student consultants so everyone can inspect notes, timelines, and dashboards without altering workflows.

Database Hygiene & Sync Standards

  • Unique Email Key: Always verify existing records via global search before creating a new Contact or Company.
  • Google Sheets Data Sync: Never rename Row 1 column headers in synced spreadsheets, or the live connection to HubSpot breaks.
  • Contact Type Segmentation: Tag every record cleanly as Prospective Client, Active Client, Past Client, or CSG Alumni.

PART A · SERVICE DATA FOUNDATIONS & THE GOLDEN DATA RULE

Before creating tickets, portals, or feedback surveys, every consultant must understand how HubSpot’s four core objects connect—and how CSG documents active and completed client engagements inside the CRM.

Object 01

Contact

The individual human sponsor (e.g., Ilija Vrajich). The Email Address is the unique Primary Key that prevents duplicate records across semesters.

Object 02

Company

The client organization (e.g., Alcomy). Stores firmographics, the CSG Client Health Score, pinned executive project notes, and historical engagement files.

Object 03

Deal

The commercial engagement (e.g., $2,500 SOP Engagement). Tracks contract fee and triggers automated Service Hub onboarding upon reaching Closed Won.

Object 04

Ticket

Operational project delivery and client communication. Tracks every scope question, data request, and deliverable sign-off inside the Help Desk and Customer Portal.

01. Help Desk (Centralized Support & Ticket Pipelines)

Core Support Workspace

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: A multi-channel support workspace that aggregates incoming inquiries from shared emails, web forms, live chats, and portals into structured tickets. Behind the scenes, HubSpot links every ticket simultaneously to Contact, Company, and Deal records while automatically measuring Time to First Reply, Time to Close, and SLA countdown timers.

CSG Specific Application: Replaces fragmented, private student email inboxes with one institutional team workspace (client-support@cougarstrategy.org), guaranteeing zero context loss across rotating semester cohorts.

Concrete CSG Use Cases
  • Scope-Change Tracking (e.g., Alcomy): Log mid-semester client requests as tickets to document what falls inside vs. outside the signed Engagement Letter.
  • Data-Delivery Follow-Ups: Set tickets awaiting client KPIs to Waiting on Client; an automated 4-day reminder prompts the lead to follow up.
  • Seamless Cohort Handover: Incoming teams open any Company record and inspect every historical ticket, blocker, and resolution.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Step 1 — Connect Inbox, Pipeline & SLAs:
    • Go to Settings (gear icon) > Inbox & Help Desk > Help Desk, click Connect a channel > Team Email, and enable automatic ticket creation.
    • Under Settings > Objects > Tickets > Pipelines, configure CSG stages: New Request → Under Review → In Progress → Waiting on Client → Resolved.
    • Under SLAs, set a 24-hour first-response target (Mon–Fri) with an automated 3-hour warning alert. Save reusable templates under CRM > Snippets (e.g., type #datarequest inside any reply).
  2. Step 2 — Inputting Information & Creating Tickets:
    • Automated Input: Inbound emails to the shared address or website support form submissions generate tickets automatically.
    • Manual Input: After verbal Zoom calls, open Service > Help Desk > Create Ticket, enter a clear title (e.g., Alcomy – Phase 2 Org Data Request), set Priority, and associate the Company and Contact.
  3. Step 3 — Daily Execution & Collaboration: Filter by Unassigned Tickets each morning, assign a Ticket Owner, reply directly via the Help Desk editor, and use the yellow Comment tab with @mentions for internal notes.
Pitfall to Avoid: Never reply to client tickets from personal Gmail/Outlook. Doing so leaves the Help Desk ticket open, turns the SLA timer red, and hides your reply from the team.

02. Customer Success (Account Health & Escalation Control)

Executive Portfolio Visibility

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: A strategic relationship-management workspace that combines custom health scoring properties, lifecycle milestone tracking, standardized onboarding/offboarding playbooks, and automated risk alerts into a single executive overview—focusing on overall account health rather than individual support tickets.

CSG Specific Application: Gives Faculty Advisor Paul Godfrey and VPs of Operations real-time visibility into every active semester project across CSG’s 3-phase delivery cadence (Phase 1 Diagnostic Assessment, Phase 2 Org Design & Strategy, Phase 3 Final SOP Blueprint).

Concrete CSG Use Case
  • Stage-Gate Early-Warning System: If a team receives no client data by Week 6 and engagement cools, Health shifts to Yellow – At Risk, enabling leadership intervention before Stage-Gate reviews.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Step 1 — Create Custom Health & Milestone Properties:
    • Go to Settings > Properties > Company Properties > Create Property.
    • Create CSG Client Health Score (Dropdown: Green – On Track, Yellow – At Risk, Red – Critical).
    • Create Engagement Milestone Status (Dropdown: Kickoff Completed, Phase 1 Diagnostic Delivered, Phase 2 Strategy Delivered, Phase 3 Final SOP Delivered).
  2. Step 2 — Weekly Friday Ritual & Data Input: Every Friday, project leads open CRM > Companies, update the Health Score and Milestone Status, pin a 3-line summary under Notes (1. Progress this week | 2. Blockers | 3. Next milestone), and set milestone reminder Tasks.
  3. Step 3 — Build Automated Escalation Workflow: Under Automations > Workflows > Company-based, trigger an instant alert to the VP of Operations and create a 48-hour sponsor alignment Task whenever CSG Client Health Score becomes Yellow or Red.
HubSpot Academy: Understanding Customer Success Management in HubSpot → Search keywords: Customer Success Management, Account Health

03. Customer Agent (Breeze AI Frontline Assistant)

AI Conversational Support

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: An AI-powered conversational assistant driven by HubSpot Breeze and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). It answers visitor questions 24/7 by drawing strictly from approved Knowledge Base articles, PDFs, and web URLs, and executes a structured human handoff when complex or custom questions arise.

CSG Specific Application: Provides instant, accurate answers on CSG’s 12–14 week semester project timelines, consulting scope boundaries, and NDA/IP policies while students are in class, and serves as a fast internal SOP lookup assistant for student consultants.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Step 1 — Configure Strict RAG Guardrails: Navigate to Service > Customer Agent > Configuration and restrict responses exclusively to approved CSG knowledge sources so the AI never invents pricing, timelines, or contractual commitments.
  2. Step 2 — Input & Sync CSG Knowledge Sources: Connect your public Knowledge Base and upload approved PDFs: the CSG Capabilities Deck, Standard Semester Project Timeline, and Engagement Scope & IP Overview.
  3. Step 3 — Configure Human Handoff Rules: Set escalation triggers so that whenever a visitor asks for a custom quote or asks a question outside approved sources, the agent captures Name and Work Email, creates a Help Desk ticket, and alerts the Business Development Lead.
Pitfall to Avoid: Never connect unvetted internal drafts or unrestricted public web search to the Customer Agent; re-sync uploaded PDFs at the start of every semester.
HubSpot Academy: Leveraging AI with HubSpot Breeze & Customer Agents → Search keywords: Breeze Customer Agent, HubSpot AI

04. Chatflows (Rule-Based Visitor Routing & Triage)

Automated 3-Way Qualification

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: Rule-based interactive website chat widgets built on an If/Then decision tree. Visitor button selections write directly to CRM Contact properties, segment inquiries automatically, and embed live calendar booking links directly inside the chat window.

CSG Specific Application: Triages CSG’s three distinct website audiences within 5 seconds—Prospective Corporate Clients, BYU Student Applicants, and CSG Alumni—so student recruiting inquiries never inflate sales pipeline metrics.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Step 1 — Create Website Bot: Go to Service > Chatflows > Create Chatflow > Website, select Bot (for 24/7 automated triage), and name it CSG Website Inbound Qualifier.
  2. Step 2 — Build the 3-Path Decision Tree & CRM Property Mapping:
    • Path A — “Hire CSG for a Consulting Project”: Prompts for company size & industry (writes to Target Client Segment for Marketing Team 1), captures Name, Work Email, and Company Name, sets Lifecycle Stage = MQL & Contact Type = Prospective Client, and opens the embedded 20-minute BD discovery Book a Meeting calendar.
    • Path B — “Apply as a Student Consultant”: Shares the BYU recruiting page and info-session dates, setting Lifecycle Stage = Other to protect sales conversion metrics.
    • Path C — “CSG Alumni Network”: Captures updated email/role, sets Contact Type = CSG Alumni, and creates a follow-up task for the Alumni Coordinator.
  3. Step 3 — Targeting & Launch: Under Targeting, display the widget on / and /services, style in CSG Navy/Gold under Display, and click Publish.

05. Knowledge Base (Public Client FAQs & Internal SOP Wiki)

Dual External & Internal Library

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: A searchable self-service content management system (CMS) for organizing and publishing help articles, process documentation, FAQs, and multimedia guides. Supports hierarchical folders, SEO/AEO indexing, granular access controls (public vs. password-protected), and search-gap analytics.

CSG Specific Application: Serves a dual institutional role: Publicly, it educates prospective B2B clients on CSG’s 7Rs framework, 3-phase project cadence, and NDA/IP protections. Privately (password-protected), it acts as CSG’s permanent internal operating wiki storing HubSpot guides, 2-minute Loom walkthroughs, and templates across cohorts.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Step 1 — Domain & Brand Configuration: Open Service > Knowledge Base > Customize Template, apply CSG branding (Navy, Gold, White), and connect your subdomain (e.g., help.cougarstrategy.org) under Settings > Domains.
  2. Step 2 — Organize Categories & Author Standardized Articles:
    • Create three core folders under Manage Categories: Client Onboarding & Process, CSG Frameworks & 7Rs, and Internal Consultant SOPs.
    • Click Create Article and follow the CSG article template: 2-Sentence Summary → Numbered Steps → Embedded 2-Minute Loom Walkthrough → Related Links.
  3. Step 3 — Access Control & Monthly Search-Gap Review: In the right sidebar under Control Audience Access, set client articles to Public and internal SOPs to Private – Password Protected. Review Knowledge Base > Insights > Search terms with no results monthly to add missing guides.
HubSpot Academy: Service Hub Software Certification — Creating a Knowledge Base → Search keywords: Knowledge Base Setup, Publishing Help Articles

06. Customer Portal (Authenticated Client Transparency Cockpit)

Executive Sponsor Portal

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: A secure, web-based login portal where external clients authenticate with their own credentials to track real-time ticket statuses, review communication history, and submit new project requests—connected directly to your Help Desk database.

CSG Specific Application: Gives executive client sponsors (CEOs and VPs at partner firms like Alcomy) full visibility into active deliverable tickets and project milestones. Elevates CSG’s consulting professionalism, reinforces scope boundaries, and eliminates repetitive “What is the status?” emails.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Step 1 — Portal Domain & Theme Setup: Go to Settings > Inbox & Help Desk > Customer Portal, connect your subdomain (e.g., portal.cougarstrategy.org), and brand the login screen and header.
  2. Step 2 — Configure Ticket Visibility Rules: Under General > Ticket Permissions, enable Allow customers to view all tickets associated with their company (so CEOs/sponsors see every workstream ticket) and restrict visible pipelines strictly to CSG Client Deliverables & Support.
  3. Step 3 — Automated Client Invitation & Portal Updates:
    • Trigger an automated portal invitation workflow when a Deal moves to Closed Won (or open CRM > Contacts and click Send Customer Portal Invitation manually).
    • Post milestone updates (Phase 1 Report, Phase 2 Org Design, Phase 3 Final Deck) as standard Help Desk replies so they appear in the client’s portal timeline, keeping internal team notes in yellow Comments.

07. Feedback Surveys (CSAT, NPS & Historical CSV Import)

Client Listening & Testimonials

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: An automated survey engine for building, distributing, and analyzing CSAT (Customer Satisfaction), NPS (Net Promoter Score, 0–10), and custom surveys via email or web link, writing every score and comment directly onto the respondent’s CRM Contact and Company records.

CSG Specific Application: Standardizes mid-semester pulse checks (Week 7 CSAT) and post-engagement evaluations (NPS), supplies Marketing Team 2 with verified proof points and quotable testimonials, and consolidates historical survey spreadsheets into one database.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Step 1 — Build & Automate Native Surveys:
    • Go to Service > Feedback Surveys > Create Survey and configure a Week 7 CSAT check-in and an end-of-semester NPS survey triggered 3 days after final presentation.
    • Testimonial & Recovery Workflows: If Last NPS Rating ≥ 9, notify Marketing Team 2 to request a case study quote; if ≤ 6, alert the VP of Operations immediately.
  2. Step 2 — Inputting Pre-Existing / Historical Surveys (CSV Import):
    • Create Properties: Under Settings > Properties > Contact Properties, create Historical NPS Score (Number), Historical Client Feedback (Multi-line text), and Project Semester (Dropdown).
    • Prepare CSV: Format past Google Forms, Qualtrics, or Excel exports with Email (mandatory unique key), First Name, Last Name, Company Name, and the matching survey columns.
    • Import & Deduplicate: Go to CRM > Contacts > Import > File from computer, map the columns, and select Create and update contacts so historical scores attach cleanly to existing client profiles.
