The Golden Data Rule: 4 Linked CRM Objects

Core Database Logic

Everything in HubSpot revolves around four connected record types. Never store client updates in private spreadsheets or personal Gmail threads—always link records together:

Contacts (People)

Individual people (CEOs, VPs, Alumni). Tracked by email address. Use Contact Type (Prospective Client, Active Client, Past Client, CSG Alumni) to segment audiences.

Companies (Organizations)

The client organization (e.g., Alcomy). Automatically groups all contacts, deals, notes, and support tickets belonging to that business.

Deals (Projects & Revenue)

Tracks potential and active consulting engagements from Discovery Call to Closed Won ($2,500+ engagement scope, timeline, and deliverables).

Tickets (Service & Support)

Tracks ongoing client questions, data requests, scope changes, and post-project support items inside the Service Hub Help Desk.

The 3-Column Record Layout: Whenever you open any Contact, Company, Deal, or Ticket in HubSpot, the screen has three columns: Left Column (Properties): Fixed fields like Revenue, Industry, Health Score, and Milestone Status. Middle Column (Timeline): Chronological log of emails, Zoom summaries, Pinned Notes, and Tasks. Right Column (Associations): Connected contacts, companies, deals, tickets, and attached files (e.g., signed Engagement Letters).

1. Help Desk (Central Support & Ticket Workspace)

−
1 What is the Function & What Does It Do?
General Functionality

The Help Desk is a centralized, multi-channel workspace that aggregates incoming inquiries from shared team emails, website forms, live chat, and portals into structured, trackable support tickets. Beyond simple inbox management, it links every ticket simultaneously to CRM Contact, Company, and Deal records, enforces Service Level Agreements (SLAs) with automated countdown timers, routes issues based on custom rules, and includes Pro AI features (automated ticket summaries, AI reply suggestions, and automated ticket naming/routing).

CSG Specific Application & Real Examples
  • Zero Knowledge Loss Across Semesters: Replaces fragmented private student email inboxes with one shared team inbox (client-support@cougarstrategy.org). When cohorts change, the entire client communication history stays permanently attached to the Company record.
  • Scope-Change Tracking (e.g., Alcomy Project): When a client requests extra analysis mid-semester, logging it as a ticket documents whether the request falls inside or outside the signed Engagement Letter scope.
  • Data Delivery Follow-Ups: When waiting on client data, setting a ticket to Waiting on Client triggers an automatic reminder after 3–4 days so projects do not stall before Stage Gate reviews.
2 How to Implement, Use & Input Our Information (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Connect Team Inbox & Configure Ticket Pipeline
  1. Click the Settings gear icon (top right) → go to Inbox & Help Desk > Help Desk.
  2. Click Connect a channel → select Team Email, connect client-support@cougarstrategy.org, and turn on Automatic ticket creation for incoming emails.
  3. Navigate to Settings > Objects > Tickets > Pipelines and customize your stages to match CSG operations: New Request → Under Review → In Progress → Waiting on Client → Resolved / Closed.
Step 2: Configure SLAs & Pipeline Automation (Pro Features)
  1. Set SLAs: Go to Settings > Inbox & Help Desk > SLAs. Set Time to First Response = 24 hours (Monday–Friday) and Time to Close by priority, with an automated warning alert 3 hours before expiration.
  2. Add Pipeline Automation: Under the ticket pipeline Automate tab, add a rule: if a ticket stays in Waiting on Client for 3 business days without reply, automatically create a follow-up Task for the Ticket Owner.
  3. Create Text Snippets: Go to CRM > Snippets and save standard templates (e.g., #datarequest). Typing #datarequest inside any ticket reply inserts the full message automatically.
Step 3: Inputting Information & Daily Team Execution
  • Automated Input: Emails to the shared inbox or website support forms automatically create a ticket linked to the sender's Contact and Company.
  • Manual Input (Calls/Meetings): Open Service > Help Desk, click Create ticket (top right), enter a standardized name ([Client] – [Topic] – [Phase]), select Pipeline, Stage, and Priority (Low/Medium/High), and associate the Company and Contact.
  • Daily Execution: Filter by Unassigned each morning, assign a Ticket Owner, reply directly using the Reply tab (client-visible), or switch to the yellow Comment tab to tag teammates or Paul Godfrey with @Name internally.
Common Pitfall to Avoid: Never reply to client tickets from your personal university Gmail/Outlook account. If you do, the ticket stays open in Help Desk, the SLA timer turns red, and future semesters lose the reply. Always reply directly inside Help Desk.

2. Customer Success (Engagement Health & Escalation Control)

−
1 What is the Function & What Does It Do?
General Functionality

Customer Success is a strategic account-management framework designed to monitor ongoing client relationship health (Account Health Score), track structured onboarding and delivery milestones, and trigger early interventions when engagement risks appear—rather than reacting only after a client complains.

CSG Specific Application
  • Real-Time Leadership Visibility: Gives Faculty Advisor Paul Godfrey and VPs of Operations an immediate traffic-light overview (Green / Yellow / Red) of every active semester engagement before Stage Gate reviews.
  • Standardized Phase Tracking: Tracks progress across CSG's three standard consulting phases: Phase 1: Diagnostic Assessment, Phase 2: Strategy & Org Design, and Phase 3: Final SOP & Slide Deck.
Important License Note (Pro vs. Enterprise Workaround): The dedicated menu item Service > Customer Success and native automated health score calculations are Enterprise-only features. With CSG's Service Hub Professional license, we replicate this exact capability using Custom Company Properties + Saved CRM Views + Escalation Workflows (steps below).
2 How to Implement, Use & Input Our Information (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Create Custom Health & Milestone Properties
  1. Go to Settings > Properties and select Company Properties.
  2. Click Create property and create two standardized dropdown fields:
    • CSG Client Health Score (Dropdown options: Green - On Track, Yellow - At Risk, Red - Critical).
    • Project Milestone Status (Dropdown options: Kickoff Completed, Phase 1 Delivered, Phase 2 Delivered, Final Presentation Delivered).
  3. Set both fields as required during client onboarding so no active client record stays blank.
Step 2: Input Weekly Client Status Updates
  1. Open CRM > Companies and select your active client record (e.g., Alcomy).
  2. In the left sidebar under CSG Client Health, update the CSG Client Health Score and Project Milestone Status after every weekly client status call, then click Save.
  3. In the middle timeline, click Notes, log a 3-bullet summary of the weekly call, and click Actions > Pin note so leadership sees the latest status at the very top.
Step 3: Build Saved Risk Views & Escalation Workflows
  1. In CRM > Companies, click + Add view, filter by CSG Client Health Score is any of Yellow - At Risk, Red - Critical, and save it as At-Risk CSG Engagements.
  2. Under Automations > Workflows, build a Company-based workflow: whenever CSG Client Health Score changes to Yellow or Red, automatically create a high-priority Task for the VP of Operations to schedule an intervention call with the client sponsor.
