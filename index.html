Why HubSpot Matters for CSG

Strategic Purpose

1. Justify ROI

Proves software return on investment and active pipeline value directly to Faculty Advisor Paul Godfrey and leadership.

2. Stop Brain Drain

Prevents client history, notes, and contacts from disappearing when student cohorts graduate or rotate each semester.

3. Dependable Pipeline

Makes CSG easier to understand, recommend, and hire through structured lead capture and professional follow-ups.

4. Connected Teams

Bridges Marketing, Sales, and Service so every form submission, deal, and project ticket lives on one shared client record.

The Golden Data Rule: 4 Linked CRM Objects

Core Database Logic

Contacts (People)

Individual people (CEOs, VPs, Alumni) tracked by email address and segmented via Contact Type (Prospective Client, Active Client, Past Client, CSG Alumni).

Companies (Organizations)

The client organization (e.g., Alcomy). Automatically groups all contacts, deals, notes, and support tickets belonging to that business.

Deals (Projects & Revenue)

Tracks consulting engagements from Discovery Call to Closed Won ($2,500+ engagement scope, timeline, and deliverables).

Tickets (Service & Support)

Tracks ongoing client inquiries, data requests, scope changes, and post-project support inside the Service Hub Help Desk.

The 3-Column Record Layout: Every Contact, Company, Deal, and Ticket displays three columns: Left Column (Properties): Fixed fields like Revenue, Industry, Health Score, and Milestone Status. Middle Column (Timeline): Chronological log of emails, call summaries, Pinned Notes, and Tasks. Right Column (Associations): Connected contacts, companies, deals, tickets, and attached files (e.g., signed Engagement Letters).

1. Help Desk (Central Support & Inquiry Workspace)

−
1 What is the Function & How It Works
General Functionality

The Help Desk is the central workspace and communication hub for all incoming client and project inquiries. Instead of handling messages in cluttered individual email inboxes, Help Desk consolidates all channels (email, website forms, live chat) in one place and automatically converts every inquiry into a structured ticket. Each ticket moves through defined processing stages (pipelines), has a priority level, an assigned owner, and tracks response times via Service Level Agreements (SLAs).

Specific Application & Value for CSG
  • Zero Knowledge Loss Across Semester Transitions: Replaces private student email inboxes with a shared team inbox (client-support@...). When consultants rotate at the end of the semester, the entire communication history remains seamlessly stored on the Company record in the CRM.
  • Structured Project Execution: All scope-change requests, data requests, or mid-project client questions during a consulting engagement are logged as tickets. Team leads see immediately which requests are open and who is handling them.
2 Step-by-Step Guide: Setup, Usage & Data Input
Step 1: Connect Team Inbox & Set Up Ticket Stages
  1. In HubSpot, click the Settings gear icon (top right) → Inbox & Help Desk > Help Desk.
  2. Click Connect a channel and choose Team Email.
  3. Enter the central CSG contact email address and enable Automatic ticket creation for incoming emails.
  4. Navigate to Settings > Objects > Tickets > Pipelines and customize the stages to fit CSG's daily project workflow: New Request → Under Review → In Progress → Waiting on Client → Resolved / Closed.
  5. Pro Addition — Configure SLAs & Pipeline Automation: Under Settings > Inbox & Help Desk > SLAs, define Time to First Response and Time to Close by priority with warning alerts before deadlines. Under the pipeline Automate tab, set an automatic follow-up Task if a ticket remains in Waiting on Client for 3 days without a reply.
Step 2: Input Information & Create Tickets
  • Automatic: Every email sent to the team address or every completed support/project form on the CSG website automatically generates a ticket linked to the Contact and Company.
  • Manual (e.g., after phone calls or Zoom calls):
    1. Open Service > Help Desk and click Create ticket in the top right.
    2. Assign a clear ticket name (e.g., Alcomy – Additional Data Delivery Phase 2).
    3. Select the Pipeline and current Stage.
    4. Set the Priority (Low, Medium, High).
    5. Under Associated records, link the corresponding Company and Contact so the ticket appears in the client file.
Step 3: Daily Ticket Handling & Team Collaboration
  1. Every morning, open Service > Help Desk and filter on the left by Unassigned tickets.
  2. Click into the ticket and assign it via the Ticket Owner field to the responsible student consultant.
  3. Reply to the Client: Use the email editor directly inside the ticket window to reply to the client—the reply is logged automatically.
  4. Internal Collaboration: Click the Comment (Internal Note) tab (highlighted in yellow) in the editor and use @Name to ask teammates or Paul Godfrey for advice without sending an email to the client.
3 Permissions & Academy Links
Permissions: With a Core Seat (View-Only), you can read tickets on the Company record. Creating, assigning, and replying requires a Service Seat (or appropriate write permissions).

2. Customer Success (Client Care & Account Health)

−
1 What is the Function & How It Works
General Functionality

The Customer Success area is a strategic control cockpit for ongoing client relationships. It enables teams to monitor the "health status" (Account Health) of clients using measurable criteria, track milestones across onboarding and project execution, and initiate early countermeasures when problems threaten to arise.

Specific Application & Value for CSG
  • Real-Time Overview for Leadership: Faculty Advisor (Paul Godfrey) and VPs of Operations see at a glance which active semester projects are in the green and where friction exists (e.g., delayed client data delivery or dissatisfaction before a Stage Gate review).
  • Standardized Project Milestones: Makes progress across the three typical CSG project phases (Phase 1: Diagnostic Assessment, Phase 2: Strategy & Org Design, Phase 3: Final SOP & Slide Deck) transparent for everyone.
Pro License Note (Workaround): The native Service > Customer Success workspace menu and automated health scores are Enterprise features. With CSG's Service Hub Professional license, use the exact Custom Properties + Saved CRM Views + Escalation Task Workflow steps below.
2 Step-by-Step Guide: Setup, Usage & Data Input
Step 1: Create Custom Health Properties
  1. Go to Settings > Properties and select Company Properties.
  2. Click Create property and create two central control fields:
    • CSG Client Health Score (Field type: Dropdown select with options: Green - On Track, Yellow - At Risk, Red - Critical).
    • Project Milestone Status (Field type: Dropdown select with options: Kickoff Completed, Phase 1 Delivered, Phase 2 Delivered, Final Presentation Delivered).
Step 2: Input Weekly Status Data
  1. Open CRM > Companies and open the record of the active client.
  2. Scroll down the left sidebar to the CSG Client Health section.
  3. After every weekly client call, update the CSG Client Health Score and Project Milestone Status, then click Save at the bottom.
  4. In the middle pane under Notes, write a short summary of the status call and click Pin so the current status remains visible at the very top.
Step 3: Use Escalation View & Workflow
  1. Open CRM > Companies and create a saved CRM view named Active CSG Clients (or At-Risk CSG Clients).
  2. Filter by CSG Client Health Score = Yellow or Red.
  3. Whenever a project moves to Yellow or Red, create a Task (manually or via a Pro Workflow) for the VP of Operations (e.g., Schedule intervention call with client CEO).
3 Permissions & Academy Links
Permissions: In a Core Seat, status values and notes can be viewed (and updated when CRM write permissions are enabled). Automated health score calculations require Enterprise; escalation workflows require a Service Hub Pro Seat.

3. Customer Agent (AI Support Assistant / Breeze AI)

−
1 What is the Function & How It Works
General Functionality

The Customer Agent is an AI-powered conversational agent (based on HubSpot Breeze AI). It autonomously searches your connected Knowledge Base, website pages, and uploaded documents to answer incoming visitor or client questions 24/7 in natural language. When the AI detects a complex inquiry or direct project request, it automatically hands off the conversation to a human consultant (Human Handoff).

Specific Application & Value for CSG
  • Relief From Repetitive Questions: Answers recurring prospect questions (e.g., "How long does a CSG project take?", "What does a consulting engagement cost?", "Which industries do you serve?") immediately and accurately without a student having to write emails manually.
  • Internal Knowledge Assistant: Can also be used internally so new consultants quickly find answers to questions about CSG standards or templates.
2 Step-by-Step Guide: Setup, Usage & Data Input
Step 1: Activate AI Agent & Connect Knowledge Sources
  1. Navigate to Service > Customer Agent and click Get Started.
  2. Assign a name (e.g., CSG Virtual Assistant) and select the communication tone (Professional, Helpful, Concise).
  3. Under Data Sources, select:
    • Your published Knowledge Base articles (see Module 5 — publish 15–20 solid articles first).
    • Your public CSG website URLs.
    • Upload approved PDF documents (e.g., the CSG Capabilities Deck or the Project Scope One-Pager).
Step 2: Define Human Handoff Rules
  1. Open the Handoff tab inside Customer Agent.
  2. Define what happens when the AI cannot answer a question with certainty or when the user selects "Speak with a consultant":
    • Select Create ticket in Help Desk and assign it to the Business Development Lead.
  3. Enable email and in-app notifications so the team is alerted immediately whenever human intervention is needed.
Step 3: Ongoing Maintenance & AI Training
  1. Once a week, go to Service > Customer Agent > Unhandled Questions.
  2. Review the list of questions where the AI had to pass.
  3. Click Add answer to Knowledge Base directly next to the question, write the approved response, and save—from that moment on, the agent knows that topic.

Permissions: Configuration and training require a Service Seat (as well as HubSpot Credits for Breeze AI).

4. Chatflows (Website Chat & Automated Lead Bots)

−
1 What is the Function & How It Works
General Functionality

Chatflows is a visual builder for chat widgets on your website. You can use it to create either a direct Live Chat (for real-time conversations) or a rule-based Chatbot (decision tree) that greets website visitors, asks targeted qualification questions, collects contact data, and books calendar meetings directly.

Specific Application & Value for CSG
  • 24/7 Lead Qualification on the CSG Website: Captures interested business decision-makers visiting the CSG website, asks about project scope and industry, and lets them immediately book a 20-minute Discovery Call.
  • Clean Separation of Visitor Groups: Immediately separates website visitors into businesses (prospective clients), students (CSG applicants), and alumni.
2 Step-by-Step Guide: Setup, Usage & Data Input
Step 1: Create Chatflow & Select Channel
  1. Go to Service > Chatflows and click Create chatflow in the top right.
  2. Select Website and choose Bot (automated sequence—recommended for student teams with class schedules).
  3. Give the chatflow a clear name (e.g., CSG Website Inbound Qualifier).
Step 2: Build Decision Tree & Data Capture
  1. Welcome Message: Enter as the first action: "Welcome to the Cougar Strategy Group! How can we help you today?"
  2. Branching Question (Quick Replies): Create three selection buttons:
    • Button 1: Request a Project (Hire CSG)
    • Button 2: Apply as a Student (Join CSG)
    • Button 3: General Question
  3. Data Capture (Required Fields for Button 1): Add sequential question nodes that write directly into CRM properties:
    • "What is your first and last name?" → Saves to First Name / Last Name.
    • "What is your business email address?" → Saves to Email.
    • "Which company do you work for?" → Saves to Company Name.
  4. Embed Meeting Booking: At the end of the branch, add the Book a meeting action and link the calendar of the CSG Business Development Lead.
Step 3: Define Target Pages & Publish
  1. Switch to the Targeting tab at the top.
  2. Specify on which URLs the chat window should appear (e.g., on the homepage and under /services, but not on internal pages).
  3. Under Display, match the colors to CSG branding (Navy Blue/Gold) and click Publish in the top right.

5. Knowledge Base (Self-Service Library & Internal SOPs)

−
1 What is the Function & How It Works
General Functionality

The Knowledge Base is a structured online library for help articles, guides, FAQs, and documentation. Articles can be organized into categories, optimized for search engines (SEO/AEO), and made either publicly accessible to all visitors or password-protected for specific user groups.

Specific Application & Value for CSG
  • External (Public): Explains to prospective clients how CSG works, consulting frameworks (e.g., the 7Rs model), project workflows, and data privacy standards—building trust and professionalism.
  • Internal (Password-Protected): Serves as a central digital handbook for new CSG consultants (SOPs, template explanations, onboarding checklists) so no institutional know-how is ever lost.
2 Step-by-Step Guide: Setup, Usage & Data Input
Step 1: Set Up Design & Domain
  1. Go to Service > Knowledge Base.
  2. Click Customize template to upload the CSG logo and set brand colors (Navy Blue, Gold, White).
  3. Under Settings > Domains, connect your subdomain (e.g., help.cougarstrategy.org).
Step 2: Create Categories & Write Articles
  1. Click Manage categories and create clear folder structures:
    • For Clients: Project Process & Onboarding
    • For Clients: CSG Methods & Frameworks
    • Internal: CSG Consultant SOPs & HubSpot Guides
  2. Click Create article in the top right.
  3. Enter a clear title (e.g., How Does a CSG Diagnostic Assessment Phase Work?).
  4. Use the rich-text editor to populate the article with subheadings, bullet points, screenshots, and embedded Loom/YouTube videos.
Step 3: Control Audience Access & Publish
  1. In the right sidebar under Control audience access, select:
    • Public for client FAQs.
    • Private – Password-protected or registration required for internal student SOPs.
  2. Click Publish. Under the Insights tab, the team can later see exactly which articles are read most frequently and where clients still have open questions.

6. Customer Portal (Protected Client Portal)

−
1 What is the Function & How It Works
General Functionality

The Customer Portal is a secure login area on your website where external clients log in with their own credentials. Inside, they see a clear list of all their open tickets, can track current processing status, send messages to the consulting team, and access direct links to the Knowledge Base.

Specific Application & Value for CSG
  • Maximum Transparency & Professionalism for Sponsors: Executive project sponsors (such as CEOs or VPs at client companies) no longer have to email asking, "What is the current status of our SOP draft?" They log in and see all open workstreams and responses live.
  • Clear Communication Boundaries: All official project updates run centrally through the portal instead of scattered individual emails.
2 Step-by-Step Guide: Setup, Usage & Data Input
Step 1: Configure Portal & Customize Branding
  1. Go to Settings > Inbox & Help Desk > Customer Portal.
  2. Connect the portal to your domain (e.g., portal.cougarstrategy.org).
  3. Under the Theme tab, customize the login screen and header with CSG branding.
Step 2: Define Ticket Visibility Rules
  1. Open the General tab.
  2. Under Ticket permissions, choose which tickets the client sees after logging in:
    • Select All tickets associated with the customer's company (ideal for CEOs/sponsors so they can oversee every workstream of their project).
  3. Select which ticket pipelines may be displayed in the portal (e.g., CSG Client Deliverables & Support, excluding purely internal admin pipelines).
Step 3: Invite Clients & Daily Usage
  1. Open CRM > Contacts, select the client's primary point of contact, and click Actions > Send customer portal invitation on the right.
  2. When student consultants work on a ticket in Help Desk, every regular email reply automatically appears as thread history in the Customer Portal—internal notes (Comments) remain invisible to the client.

7. Feedback Surveys (Satisfaction Surveys & Importing Existing Surveys)

−
1 What is the Function & How It Works
General Functionality

The Feedback Surveys module creates, distributes, and analyzes standardized client satisfaction surveys:

  • CSAT (Customer Satisfaction): Measures satisfaction after a concrete milestone or project completion (e.g., "How satisfied were you with Phase 1?").
  • NPS (Net Promoter Score): Measures willingness to recommend on a 0–10 scale ("How likely are you to recommend CSG?").
  • Custom Surveys: Individual questionnaires with free-text fields, multiple-choice, and star ratings. All responses are automatically stored on the Contact and Company record.
Specific Application & Value for CSG
  • Central Foundation for Marketing Team 2: Team 2 investigates what went well or poorly in past CSG projects and gathers proof points. With Feedback Surveys, all ratings and quotes live in one central place instead of scattered Google Forms or Excel sheets.
  • Automated Testimonial Collection: If a client rates the project 9 or 10, HubSpot can automatically send a thank-you email asking permission to publish their feedback as a quote on the CSG website.
2 Step-by-Step Guide: Setup, Usage & Importing Existing Data
Part A: Create a New Automated Survey
  1. Go to Service > Feedback Surveys and click Create survey.
  2. Select the type: Customer satisfaction (CSAT), Customer loyalty (NPS), or Custom survey.
  3. Select the delivery method: Email (for personalized links) or Shareable link (for embedding in final presentations).
  4. Customize the questions and CSG logo under the Survey tab.
  5. Configure Automation (Automation / Recipients Tab):
    • Trigger 1 (Post-Project): Send the survey automatically 3 days after the associated Deal moves to Closed Won / Project Completed.
    • Trigger 2 (End-of-Semester Check-In): Create a scheduled workflow for the final week of the semester that sends a semester-review survey to all contacts with Engagement Status = Active.
Part B: Import Existing / Historical Surveys (CSV Import)

If CSG already holds older survey results from prior semesters (e.g., from Qualtrics, Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, or Excel), import them centrally into HubSpot as follows:

  1. Create Custom Properties in HubSpot: Go to Settings > Properties > Contact Properties and create matching fields for historical data:
    • Historical NPS Score (Field type: Number)
    • Past Project CSAT Rating (Field type: Dropdown 1–5)
    • Client Feedback & Testimonial Quote (Field type: Multi-line text)
    • Survey Completion Date (Field type: Date picker)
  2. Prepare Excel / CSV File: Open your existing survey table and name the column headers cleanly: Email, First Name, Last Name, Company Name, Historical NPS Score, Client Feedback & Testimonial Quote, Survey Completion Date. Save the file as .csv (UTF-8).
  3. Run Import in HubSpot: Go to CRM > Contacts and click Import (top right) → select File from computer > One file > One object > Contacts (or Multiple objects to link Contacts and Companies simultaneously) → upload the .csv file → map each column to its corresponding HubSpot property (e.g., Rating column → Historical NPS Score) → click Finish import. HubSpot matches existing clients by email address without creating duplicates.
Reporting Note: Imported CSV values appear as regular properties on the Contact record, not in HubSpot's native Survey Analytics reports—use Custom Reports in Module 8 to chart historical values.

8. Service Analytics (Reports, Metrics & Executive Dashboards)

−
1 What is the Function & How It Works
General Functionality

Service Analytics is the specialized evaluation and reporting suite of Service Hub. It offers over 20 pre-built standard reports plus a Custom Report Builder to visually display ticket volume, average response times, resolution speeds, support workload per team member, and customer satisfaction trends (CSAT/NPS) over time.

Specific Application & Value for CSG
  • ROI & Quality Proof for Paul Godfrey: Shows faculty leadership and the board with hard numbers how quickly student teams respond to client inquiries, average client satisfaction across all projects, and which topics generate the most follow-up questions.
  • Data Foundation for Marketing Team 1 & Team 2: Combines satisfaction scores with client segments (e.g., whether tech startups or mid-market firms yield higher NPS scores) to prioritize future target audiences based on data.
2 Step-by-Step Guide: Setup, Usage & Data Input
Step 1: Review & Filter Standard Reports
  1. Navigate to Service > Service Analytics.
  2. Select the desired report category in the left menu:
    • Customer Outcomes: CSAT trend over time, NPS distribution (Promoters vs. Detractors).
    • Response Times: Average Time to First Reply, Average Resolution Duration.
    • Ticket Volume: Tickets created by category/project phase.
  3. Use the global filters at the top to narrow the timeframe to one exact semester (e.g., This quarter / Last 90 days).
Step 2: Pin Reports to the CSG Executive Dashboard
  1. Once you find an important report (e.g., NPS Score by Client Segment), click Save report in the top right of the report.
  2. Select Add to existing dashboard and choose the CSG Executive Leadership Dashboard.
  3. Repeat this for the 3 most important service metrics so Paul has all Marketing, Sales, and Service KPIs on one single overview page.
Step 3: Schedule Automated Report Delivery
  1. Open Reporting > Dashboards and select the CSG Executive Leadership Dashboard.
  2. Click Share > Email this dashboard in the top right.
  3. Enter the email addresses of Paul Godfrey and the Team Leads.
  4. Select Recurring – Monthly (on the 1st day of the month) and PDF file format. Leadership will now automatically receive an updated performance report every month.
