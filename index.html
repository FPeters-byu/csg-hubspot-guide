2. The "Golden Data Rule": How Our CRM Is Structured

Before clicking any tool, understand the four connected objects inside HubSpot. Rule: A Contact works at a Company, signs a Deal, and communicates during delivery via Tickets.

1. Contacts

Individual people (e.g., Ilija Vrajich).

Golden Rule: Email address is the unique ID. Never create a contact without an email. Always tag Contact Type (Prospective Client, Active Client, Past Client, CSG Alumni).

2. Companies

Client organizations (e.g., Alcomy).

Acts as the master folder where all contacts, deals, support tickets, pinned weekly notes, and the CSG Client Health Score live together.

3. Deals

Consulting engagements with revenue value (e.g., $2,500 Semester Engagement).

Moves across the Sales Pipeline (Discovery → Proposal Sent → Closed Won). Moving to Closed Won officially triggers Service delivery.

4. Tickets

Client requests & deliverables inside the Help Desk.

Tracks scope changes, client data requests, and milestone sign-offs with SLA response timers so nothing gets buried in personal student inboxes.

4. The 5 Golden Daily Rules for Every CSG Consultant

Follow these five habits every week to keep our CRM clean and reliable across cohorts.

  1. Never use private student email inboxes for client work: Always reply from HubSpot (Help Desk or connected inbox extension) so future cohorts can read the full thread.
  2. Friday Pinned-Note Ritual: Every Friday, project leads post a 3-line pinned note on the client's Company record (1. Progress this week | 2. Blockers/Risks | 3. Next milestone & date).
  3. Never rename Row 1 headers in synced Google Sheets: Renaming column headers breaks HubSpot Data Sync integrations.
  4. Search before creating: Always check the global search bar at the top left before creating a Contact or Company to prevent duplicates from past semesters.
  5. Document new setups with a 2-minute Loom video: Whenever you build a form, workflow, or survey, record a quick Loom walkthrough and link it in our internal Knowledge Base.

1 Help Desk (Centralized Support & Ticket Workspace) Pro Feature
▾
1. What Is the Function & What Does It Do?

General Functionality

A unified omnichannel workspace that aggregates incoming inquiries from shared emails, web forms, live chat, calling, and messaging apps into structured tickets. It links every ticket simultaneously to Contact, Company, and Deal records, enforces Service Level Agreements (SLAs), and includes Pro AI features (automated ticket summaries, naming, and routing).

CSG Specific Application

Replaces private student email inboxes with one shared team inbox (client-support@cougarstrategy.org). Tracks client questions, data requests, and scope changes during engagements so zero client context is lost when student cohorts rotate between semesters.

2. How to Implement, Use & Input Information

Step 1: Connect Team Inbox & Configure Ticket Pipeline

  1. Go to Settings (gear icon) → Inbox & Help Desk → Help Desk.
  2. Click Connect a channel → Team Email, enter the central CSG support address, and enable Automatic ticket creation.
  3. Navigate to Settings → Objects → Tickets → Pipelines and set CSG stages: New Request → Under Review → In Progress → Waiting on Client → Resolved.

Step 2: Configure SLAs & Pipeline Automation (Pro Features)

  1. SLAs: Go to Settings → Inbox & Help Desk → SLAs. Set Time to first reply = 24 business hours (Mon–Fri) tiered by priority, with automated email warnings 3 hours before expiration.
  2. Automation: Set a workflow rule that automatically creates a follow-up task if a ticket stays in Waiting on Client for more than 3–4 days without a reply.

Step 3: Inputting Tickets & Daily Team Execution

  1. Automatic Input: Emails to the team address or website support forms auto-create linked tickets.
  2. Manual Input (Calls/Zoom): Open Service → Help Desk, click Create Ticket, use the standard naming format [Client] – [Topic] – [Phase] (e.g., Alcomy – Phase 2 Data Delivery), set Priority, and associate the Company and Contact.
  3. Daily Execution: Filter by Unassigned each morning, assign a Ticket Owner, reply directly from the Help Desk editor (never personal Gmail), and use yellow Comments with @mentions for internal team or faculty advisor notes.

3 Customer Agent (Breeze AI Conversational Assistant) Breeze AI
▾
1. What Is the Function & What Does It Do?

General Functionality

An AI-powered support agent (HubSpot Breeze AI) using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). It indexes your published Knowledge Base, public website URLs, and uploaded PDFs to answer visitor questions 24/7 in natural language—while enforcing strict guardrails and handing off complex requests to humans.

CSG Specific Application

Answers routine prospect questions 24/7 (e.g., "How long is a CSG engagement?", "What is the project fee?", "How do you handle NDAs?") without student manual labor, and helps new student consultants find SOP templates during onboarding.

2. How to Implement, Use & Input Information

Step 1: Activate Agent & Upload CSG Knowledge Sources

  1. Publish 15–20 core Knowledge Base articles first (Module 5) so the AI has accurate training data.
  2. Go to Service → Customer Agent → Get Started, name it CSG Virtual Assistant, and set tone to Professional, Helpful, Concise.
  3. Under Data Sources, connect your Knowledge Base, public CSG website URLs, and upload approved PDFs (CSG Capabilities Deck, Standard Timeline & Pricing One-Pager).

Step 2: Configure Guardrails & Human Handoff Rules

  1. Enable strict grounding so the AI answers only from approved CSG documents and never guesses.
  2. Under Handoff, configure fallback routing: if a visitor asks for a custom proposal or clicks Talk to a Consultant, automatically Create a Help Desk Ticket assigned to the Business Development Lead.

Step 3: Weekly AI Training

  1. Every week, open Service → Customer Agent → Unhandled Questions.
  2. Review questions where the AI lacked confidence and click Add to Knowledge Base to publish verified answers.

7 Feedback Surveys (CSAT, NPS & Historical CSV Import) Pro Feature
▾
1. What Is the Function & What Does It Do?

General Functionality

Creates, automates, and analyzes standardized Customer Satisfaction (CSAT), Net Promoter Score (NPS, 0–10), and custom surveys via email or web link. Responses save directly to CRM Contact and Company records.

CSG Specific Application

Provides Marketing Team 2 with centralized proof points and quotable client testimonials for case studies, triggers automatic testimonial requests when clients score 9 or 10, and alerts leadership if a score is low.

2. How to Implement, Use & Import Historical Surveys

Part A: Build & Automate Native Surveys

  1. Go to Service → Feedback Surveys → Create Survey and select CSAT (post-milestone) or NPS (end-of-semester loyalty).
  2. Customize questions, add the CSG logo, and select Email or Shareable Link.
  3. Under Automation / Recipients, set Trigger 1: send automatically 3 days after Deal Stage becomes Closed Won / Project Completed.

Part B: Import Pre-Existing Historical Surveys (Qualtrics / Google Forms / Excel)

  1. Create Custom Contact Properties: Go to Settings → Properties → Contact Properties and create Historical NPS Score (Number), Past Project CSAT Rating (Dropdown 1–5), Client Feedback & Testimonial Quote (Multi-line text), and Survey Completion Date (Date).
  2. Format CSV (UTF-8): Name columns clearly: Email, First Name, Last Name, Company Name, Historical NPS Score, Client Feedback & Testimonial Quote, Survey Completion Date.
  3. Run Import: Go to CRM → Contacts → Import → File from computer → One file → Contacts, upload the CSV, map columns to your custom properties, and finish. HubSpot matches by email without creating duplicates.
Important Reporting Tip: Imported CSV survey values appear as standard Contact Properties rather than inside HubSpot's native Survey Analytics tool—use the Custom Report Builder (Module 8) to chart historical scores alongside new native surveys.
