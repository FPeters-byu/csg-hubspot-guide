01 · CRM ARCHITECTURE & GOVERNANCE

Ground Rules for every cohort

Before working inside any hub, every consultant must understand how HubSpot connects records and how CSG allocates paid seats versus free View-Only access.

The Four Core CRM Objects

  • Contact: The individual person (e.g., client CEO or sponsor). Email address is the unique primary key that prevents duplicates.
  • Company: The client organization (e.g., Alcomy). Holds long-term project history and account-level properties.
  • Deal: The paid consulting engagement ($2,500+ scope). Tracks revenue and triggers client onboarding upon Closed Won.
  • Ticket: Operational project requests, scope questions, and deliverables tracked inside the Service Hub.

License Structure & Data Hygiene

  • Paid Specialist Seats: Assigned to Sales leads, Marketing Core leads (3 seats), and the Service Hub Pro lead (1 seat).
  • Free View-Only Seats: Assigned to all remaining student consultants to view notes, histories, and dashboards without altering workflows.
  • 2,000 Marketing Contacts Cap: Mark rejected student applicants and inactive contacts as Non-Marketing Contacts to avoid overage charges.
  • Google Sheets Sync Rule: Never rename Row 1 column headers in synced spreadsheets, or the HubSpot Data Sync connection will break.

01. Help Desk

Core Support Workspace

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: A centralized multi-channel support workspace that aggregates incoming inquiries from shared emails, web forms, and live chats into structured tickets. It links every ticket simultaneously to Contact, Company, and Deal records while tracking Time to First Reply, Time to Close, and Service Level Agreement (SLA) countdowns.

CSG Specific Application: Replaces scattered, private student email inboxes with a single institutional workspace. All client questions, data requests, and scope-change discussions are logged as tickets so context is preserved across rotating semester cohorts.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Connect Inbox & Pipeline: Go to Settings > Inbox & Help Desk > Help Desk, connect the CSG team email with automatic ticket creation, and set stages: New Request → Under Review → In Progress → Waiting on Client → Resolved.
  2. Set SLAs & Snippets: Under SLAs, set a 24-hour first-response target (Mon–Fri) with a 3-hour warning. Save reusable text templates under CRM > Snippets (e.g., #datarequest).
  3. Input Tickets: Inbound team emails generate tickets automatically. For verbal call requests, click Service > Help Desk > Create Ticket, enter the title, priority, and associate the Company and Contact.
  4. Daily Execution: Filter by Unassigned Tickets, assign a Ticket Owner, reply via the Help Desk editor (never personal Gmail), or use the yellow Comment tab with @mentions for internal notes.
HubSpot Academy: Managing Tickets in the Help Desk Workspace → Search keywords: Help Desk Workspace, Ticket Pipelines

02. Customer Success

Account Health & Escalation

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: A strategic relationship management workspace that tracks overall account health scores, monitors lifecycle milestones, standardizes onboarding/offboarding playbooks, and triggers proactive interventions before client relationships deteriorate.

CSG Specific Application: Gives Faculty Advisors and VPs of Operations real-time visibility into active semester projects (Phase 1 Assessment, Phase 2 Strategy, Phase 3 Final SOP). Acts as an early-warning system if client responsiveness drops before Stage Gate reviews.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Create Health Properties: Go to Settings > Properties > Company Properties and create CSG Client Health Score (Green - On Track, Yellow - At Risk, Red - Critical) and Project Milestone Status.
  2. Weekly Friday Ritual: Every Friday, project leads open CRM > Companies, update the Health Score, and pin a 3-line summary under Notes (Progress, Blockers, Next Milestone).
  3. Build Escalation Workflow: Under Automations > Workflows, trigger an internal alert to the VP of Operations and create a 48-hour alignment task whenever Health changes to Yellow or Red.
HubSpot Academy: Understanding Customer Success Management → Search keywords: Customer Success Management, Account Health

03. Customer Agent (Breeze AI)

AI Frontline Assistant

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: An AI-powered conversational assistant driven by HubSpot Breeze and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). It answers visitor questions 24/7 using only approved documents, URLs, and Knowledge Base articles, then executes a human handoff for complex inquiries.

CSG Specific Application: Answers prospective client questions on timelines (12–14 weeks), project scope, and capabilities around the clock, and acts as an internal SOP lookup bot for new student consultants.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Configure Guardrails: Navigate to Service > Customer Agent > Configuration and restrict answers strictly to approved CSG sources so the AI never guesses pricing or terms.
  2. Upload Knowledge Sources: Connect the public Knowledge Base and upload the CSG Capabilities Deck, Standard Project Timeline Overview, and Engagement Terms.
  3. Set Human Handoff: Configure intent detection so high-intent project inquiries capture Name and Email, create a Help Desk ticket, and alert the Business Development lead.
HubSpot Academy: Leveraging AI with HubSpot Breeze & Customer Agents → Search keywords: Breeze Customer Agent, HubSpot AI

04. Chatflows

Structured Visitor Routing

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: Rule-based interactive chat widgets that follow a structured If/Then decision tree. Visitor button selections write directly to CRM contact properties, qualify inquiries, and embed live calendar booking links inside the chat window.

CSG Specific Application: Separates CSG’s three distinct website visitor groups within 5 seconds—Prospective Clients, BYU Student Applicants, and Alumni—so student applications never clog the client project pipeline.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Build Decision Tree: Go to Service > Chatflows > Create Chatflow and add 3 quick-reply buttons: Hire CSG (Client Inquiry), Join CSG (Student), and Alumni Network.
  2. Map CRM Properties: On the Client branch, prompt for First Name, Last Name, Work Email, and Company Name, saving each answer to its CRM property.
  3. Embed Booking & Publish: End the Client branch with a Book a Meeting node linked to the BD lead’s calendar, style in CSG Navy/Blue, and publish on public pages.
HubSpot Academy: Building Chatflows and Automating Conversation Routing → Search keywords: Chatflows, Website Bot Setup

05. Knowledge Base

Public FAQs & Internal SOPs

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: A structured online help library for articles, guides, and FAQs. Supports category hierarchies, search analytics, and audience access controls (public web access vs. password-protected or registered access).

CSG Specific Application: Serves a dual purpose: a public client hub explaining CSG’s 7Rs model and engagement process, and a password-protected internal wiki storing HubSpot guides, Loom walkthroughs, and templates across semesters.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Brand & Categories: Open Service > Knowledge Base, apply CSG branding, and create folders: Client Onboarding & Process, CSG Frameworks, and Internal Consultant SOPs.
  2. Author Articles: Click Create Article, structure steps with clear headings and embedded 2-minute Loom videos.
  3. Control Access: In the right sidebar under Control audience access, set client guides to Public and internal SOPs to Private / Password-Protected before publishing.
HubSpot Academy: Creating and Managing a Knowledge Base → Search keywords: Knowledge Base, Help Articles

06. Customer Portal

Client Transparency Cockpit

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: A secure, web-based portal where external clients log in with their own credentials to view real-time status updates on all open tickets, review communication threads, and submit new requests.

CSG Specific Application: Gives executive client sponsors (CEOs/VPs) full visibility into ongoing deliverable tickets and project milestones—elevating CSG’s consulting image and eliminating repetitive “What is the status?” emails.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Configure Domain & Theme: Go to Settings > Inbox & Help Desk > Customer Portal, connect your subdomain (e.g., portal.cougarstrategy.org), and apply CSG styling.
  2. Set Ticket Visibility: Under General > Ticket Permissions, allow sponsors to view All tickets associated with their Company and filter strictly to the external CSG Client Support pipeline.
  3. Automate Client Invites: Trigger an automated portal registration email whenever a Deal moves to Closed Won. Public Help Desk replies appear in the portal; internal yellow comments stay hidden.
HubSpot Academy: Setting Up a Customer Portal in HubSpot → Search keywords: Customer Portal, Client Portal

07. Feedback Surveys

CSAT, NPS & Historical Import

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: Builds, automates, and analyzes standardized Customer Satisfaction (CSAT), Net Promoter Score (NPS 0–10), and custom surveys, binding every response permanently to the respondent’s CRM Contact and Company record.

CSG Specific Application: Captures mid-semester pulse checks (Week 7 CSAT) and post-project NPS scores, automatically flags top promoters (9–10) for Marketing testimonials, and consolidates historical survey spreadsheets into the CRM.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Build Automated Surveys: Go to Service > Feedback Surveys > Create Survey. Configure a 1-click CSAT pulse check for Week 7 and an NPS survey triggered 3 days after final presentation.
  2. Testimonial Workflow: When Last NPS Rating ≥ 9, automatically notify Marketing to request permission to publish the client quote as a case study.
  3. Import Historical Surveys: Clean past CSV exports with mandatory Email column, map to custom properties (Historical NPS Score, Historical Semester), and import via CRM > Contacts > Import using Create and update contacts.
HubSpot Academy: Collecting Customer Feedback with Surveys → Search keywords: Customer Feedback Surveys, NPS CSAT HubSpot

08. Service Analytics

Executive KPIs & Segment ROI

1. What is the function & what does it do?

General Functionality: Real-time reporting engine combining ticket timestamps, SLA compliance, CSAT/NPS scores, and CRM Company properties (industry, deal value, cohort) into shareable executive dashboards.

CSG Specific Application: Proves service quality to Faculty Advisors and shows Marketing which target client segments deliver the highest NPS scores and lowest support friction.

2. How to implement, use & input our information

  1. Build 4 Mandatory Reports: Under Service > Service Analytics, add (1) Overall NPS Gauge, (2) Average NPS by Target Client Segment, (3) Average Time to First Reply, and (4) Active Client Health Donut.
  2. Save Executive Dashboard: Pin all four reports to the CSG Service & Client Outcomes dashboard.
  3. Automate Semester Export: Use Dashboards > Share > Recurring Email to send a PDF snapshot automatically to Faculty Advisors and incoming leads at semester close.
HubSpot Academy: Reporting & Service Analytics in HubSpot → Search keywords: Service Analytics, HubSpot Dashboards
